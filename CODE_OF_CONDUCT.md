# Code of conduct

MemHouse is built by people with different backgrounds, levels of experience and opinions. We want
taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and take feedback on the work, with reasons and, where you can, a better alternative.
- Make room for newcomers: answer questions patiently and explain context.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery, in public or in
  private.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Encouraging any of the above.

## Where it applies

In the project's repositories, issue trackers, discussions, chat channels and events, and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers at the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (marked "conduct")
or privately to any maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in
a report don't take part in handling it.

## What happens

Maintainers decide on a response that fits what happened: a private conversation, a written
warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Decisions are explained to the
people involved.

This code draws on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
