# Trademark policy

> **Draft for counsel.** This page is approved by the founder and counsel before the repository is
> published (roadmap goal G09), once the name clearance is done and the application is filed.

"MemHouse" and the MemHouse logo are trademarks of the project's owner. The Apache-2.0 licence
covers the code, not the names and logos (section 6 of the licence). This page says how you can
use them.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software works with, integrates with or is built on MemHouse, in plain text, as
  long as nothing suggests that the project endorses or certifies it.
- Use the name to refer to the project accurately in articles, talks, books and comparisons.
- Run and redistribute unmodified MemHouse under its name, and call a build of unmodified
  source "MemHouse".

## Please ask first

- Distributing a modified version under the MemHouse name. A modified version should have another
  name, and may say it is "based on MemHouse".
- Using the name or the logo in the name of your product, company, service or domain, or in a way
  that looks official.
- Offering MemHouse as a hosted service under the MemHouse name.
- Merchandise.

## Never

- Use the marks in a way that is misleading about who makes, supports or certifies something.
- Register confusingly similar names, domains or social accounts.
